# Content Doctrine — permanent creative standing orders

_This file is the channel owner's creative doctrine. It is injected into EVERY
content generation call, ahead of the auto-generated strategy. Unlike
`strategy.md` (rewritten daily by `strategy_loop.py`), this file is never
touched by automation — edit it by hand or by asking Claude._

---

## 1. Speak to ONE person (the ICP)

Every hook is written to ONE specific man, not an audience: **18–34, on a
self-improvement arc — gym, ambition, quiet grind. He feels behind, distracted,
softer than he wants to be. He wants structure, inner strength, and an edge.**
The more specifically a hook lands on HIM, the more it converts. Generic wisdom
("Stoic quote of the day") converts nobody.

## 2. Men respond to being QUANTIFIED

Rank, percentile, count, deadline — numbers make the stakes personal. Keep the
calm register and dignity (we challenge, never insult), and keep every word
YouTube-safe:

- RIGHT: "Most men lose this fight before 9am." / "99% never train this." /
  "One habit separates you from him." / "You've restarted 14 times."
- ALSO WORKS (feel-seen register): "It's not weakness. It's untrained." /
  "Nobody taught you this."
- WRONG: insult-framing ("you're pathetic"), hype-bro shouting, anything
  graphic or shaming — that gets suppressed and breaks the channel's voice.

## 3. Copy proven formats — don't reinvent

The algorithm already told everyone what works. Steal structures that are
already viral in the self-improvement/philosophy niche and adapt them to
Stoicism (that's how the `story` format was chosen). Never invent a format from
nothing when a proven one can be adapted.

## 4. Test NEW, then multiply the WINNER until it dies

Structurally different tests beat 1000 variations of the same idea. But the
moment a format/hook style shows strong signal in analytics, make MANY
variations of that winner and ride it until performance decays. Then find the
next. (The experiment agent + channel report exist to surface these winners —
check them before changing direction.)
